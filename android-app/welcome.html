Guest mode

Guest mode lets you use Vayun without an account. Your workspaces, pages and tasks are stored only in this app, on this phone. They are not sent to any server and are not synced to the cloud.

Your data and backups

Because nothing is stored online, Vayun cannot recover guest data for you. It can be lost if you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, reset or replace the phone, or if the phone's storage fails. Vayun does not make backups of guest data. You can save your own copy at any time from the sidebar using Backup Workspace.

Signing in later

You can sign in with Google at any time from the sidebar to use cloud features such as teams. Signing in is optional. Work you did as a guest stays on this phone unless you back it up.

Privacy

In guest mode Vayun does not collect your notes. The name you enter is saved on this phone only and is used to greet you in the app.
